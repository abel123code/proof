02
the problem

building got cheap. being seen didn't.

  • the bottleneck moved. not "can you build it," but "did anyone see it."
  • the advice every job-seeker gets: build your brand, post, make content.
  • the people who most need it won't do it. it feels cringe, the activation energy is too high.
  • so they ship something real, and it dies in a void.
03
the insight

don't make them creators. reverse-engineer what already works.

  • "AI writes you a script" is saturated. everyone can smell generic.
  • the product is judgment: which proven structure to borrow, which phrases to push.
  • learned from content genuinely performing in your niche, not a blank-prompt chatbot.
  • re-skin the structure to your project, frame it for a recruiter, post it where devs already are.
04
what it is

connect a repo. get a video you'd actually post.

you point proof at a project you're proud of. minutes later you have a recruiter-facing short-form video, and you never wrote a hook, learned pacing, or opened an editor.
  • read the script off an in-browser teleprompter
  • it cuts your filler, dead air, and retakes automatically
  • captions, keyword overlays, architecture overlays, finished MP4
▶ run the 30-second demo
05
how it works

one repo in, one MP4 out

repo→ understand→ Exa research→ reverse-engineer→ brief→ script→ teleprompter→ whisper-1→ cut engine→ Zo render→ edited.mp4
Exa at the front finds proven content in your niche. Zo at the back is a real Linux box that cuts, captions, and renders. the script you read is the ground truth that lets the render cut out mistakes cleanly.

07
why Exa and Zo are load-bearing

not bolted on. the product can't exist without them.

Exa · the research intelligence

the whole pipeline reasons over what Exa finds. swap it for a keyword scrape and every script regresses to the generic column. it's the input the product's judgment depends on.

Zo · the render runtime

Remotion drives a headless browser, ffmpeg encodes. a full Linux workload a serverless function can't hold. the render needs a real machine that stays up. that's Zo.

OpenAI runs the reasoning and whisper-1 word-level transcription. built with Codex and Cursor.
08
proof of work

it's real, and the repo shows it

  • eight-phase pipeline, one Supabase table per phase, resumable, watch-it-think UI
  • the cut engine: filler removal, dead-space trim, script-guided retake removal via sequence alignment
  • whisper-1 word-level timestamps, with an original-to-cut timeline remap so captions never drift
  • a real render service on Zo: download, transcribe, cut, render with the Remotion CLI, upload
public repo, end to end. nobody else aligns a transcript to a teleprompter script to find retakes.

  • a second surface: an AI-maintained blog and portfolio that keeps you warm between launches.
  • this is 'Sup's own thesis: help capable people get seen in a brutal job market.
